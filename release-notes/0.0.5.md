### Craft is now Cascade

This is the last version of Craft. The app continues as **Cascade**: the same app under a new name, and the one that gets updates from now on.

- After this update, Craft offers to install Cascade next to it. Craft then quits, and Cascade opens in its place.
- Cascade brings your projects, sessions and settings across on its first launch. Nothing needs exporting.
- macOS asks once more for the permissions Craft had, such as notifications and the microphone, because Cascade is a new app to it.
- Once Cascade is installed, opening Craft opens Cascade. You can move Craft to the Trash whenever you like.

If the install does not work, download Cascade from its releases page and open it. It brings your data across on its own.
